What is Ceeries?

Ceeries is a Filipino lifestyle brand founded by Riona Karl Cee. We offer stylish, practical, affordable bags, Hydra Slay Lip Oil, and scrunchies for everyday use.

How can I place an order?

Choose your shades, add them to your cart, and send a product inquiry. Lip oils are ₱199 each. This project preview saves inquiries; it does not take payments.

What shades are available for the Hydra Slay Lip Oil?

Seven shades: Gorg, Barbie, Diva, Hot, Queen, Icon, and Purr.

How do I choose the right lip oil shade?

Gorg: clear. Barbie: pink. Diva: orange. Hot: red. Queen: dark red. Icon: brown. Purr: nude. Choose the tone that suits your look.

How can I contact Ceeries?

Visit our Contact page or official Instagram, @ceeries.ph, for product questions and ordering guidance.

What products does Ceeries offer?

Ceeries offers a curated selection of bags, Hydra Slay Lip Oil, and scrunchies. Our products are designed to complement different styles, personalities, and everyday routines.

Who is Ceeries for?

Ceeries is made especially for girls who want products that are stylish, practical, and affordable—whether you’re a student, young professional, or simply someone who loves adding little things to your everyday look.

Where is Ceeries based?

Ceeries is a Filipino-owned brand serving customers in the Philippines.

Are Ceeries products affordable?

Yes! Ceeries believes that looking and feeling good shouldn’t have to be expensive. We aim to offer products that balance style, quality, functionality, and affordability.

Are Ceeries products suitable for everyday use?

Absolutely! Our products are thoughtfully selected with everyday routines in mind—from carrying your essentials to adding a little color and style to your look.

What makes Ceeries different?

Ceeries goes beyond simply offering products. We want to be part of every girl’s everyday journey, self-expression, and new beginnings by creating a collection of pieces that are easy to enjoy, use, and make your own.

What does Ceeries stand for?

Ceeries stands for confidence, individuality, and self-expression. We believe that even the little things we carry and use can reflect who we are.

Does Ceeries prioritize product quality?

Yes. Reliable quality is part of our promise. We value the trust our customers place in us, which is why we strive to provide products that customers can confidently enjoy and use.
